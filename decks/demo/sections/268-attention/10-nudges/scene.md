## Glow, pulse and shake

> Glow marks the part you talk about; breathe says "this one is live". A pulse is a quick "look here"; a shake says "this failed". Each is one step, on any element.

- The whole flow.
- The API glows.
- It breathes: live.
- A pulse on the web app.
- The database shakes: it failed.
- The glow goes off.
