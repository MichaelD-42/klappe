## Shipping a change

- Three lanes: Requester, Team, Review.
- The requester raises the change.
- The team assesses the impact.
- Review approves the change.
- The team makes the change.
- The requester accepts the result.

> Follow the dot: the change moves between people, and every hand-over is a lane change. Each hand-over is a place where work can wait.

- Can we ship? The decision, question by question.
- The tests are green.
- The review is approved: ship it.

> Two questions, asked in order. Today both answers are yes, so the change ships.
