## Hand-drawn marks

> Use a mark to point at part of a sentence, the way a pen on a whiteboard would. On a code line a ring flattens into the line gap; on dense prose, prefer box, underline or strike with a small pad. A cross says wrong; a bracket groups a run of lines.

- Ship the change only after the check passes.
- Ring the condition.
- Underline what has to pass.
- An arrow to where it starts.
- Highlight what ships.
- Friday night deploys.
- Cross it out: a cross is --k-bad unless you give it a color.
- A bracket groups both lines.
