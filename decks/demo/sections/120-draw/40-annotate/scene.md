## Hand-drawn marks

> Use a mark to point at part of a sentence, the way a pen on a whiteboard would. On a code line a ring flattens into the line gap; on dense prose, prefer box, underline or strike with a small pad.

- Ship the change only after the check passes.
- Ring the condition.
- Underline what has to pass.
- An arrow to where it starts.
- Highlight what ships.
