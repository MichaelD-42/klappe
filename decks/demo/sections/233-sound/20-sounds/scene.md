## klappe's sounds

> Each step plays one of klappe's own sounds, by the name on screen: listen through them before you pick one. The beds, bed-calm and bed-pulse, go under a section with section.ts music. src/sounds/README.md says how each was made.

- whoosh: a camera flight in
- whip: a whip pan between far scenes
- hit: a landing, with impactShake
- pop: something appears
- click: a small step
- tick: a checklist item ticks
- chime: success, a target reached
- fail: a miss
- rise: the build-up before a reveal
- shimmer: a reveal lands
