## World map

> Use the world map to show where people or sites are and how they connect.

- The land draws.
- The sites appear.
- Arcs draw to the host site.
- The camera zooms to one site.
- Back to the whole world.
- Where we sell: three countries fill in.

> region(tl, country) takes the name as world-atlas spells it ("Germany", "United States of America") or the ISO 3166-1 numeric code ("276").
