## YouTube video on cue

> Give the clip its own play step: the box waits on its poster while you set the clip up, and the next click starts it, so a clicker is all you need.

- video(ctx, { href, title, start, end, rect }), then v.play(tl) — the box shows and waits
- On the next step the player starts at 0:43, with sound; it stops at 1:00, or when you move on
