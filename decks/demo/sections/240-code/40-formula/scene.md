## Bayes' rule

> Use a formula that builds term by term for a technical training: set the frame first, then add one term per step and say what it means before the next one appears.

- The question: how likely is A, now that we have seen B?
- How likely B is when A holds.
- Times how likely A was before.
- Over how likely B is at all.
- The prior is where most mistakes start.
